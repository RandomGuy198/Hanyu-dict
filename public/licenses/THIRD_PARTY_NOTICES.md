## Datasets and assets that are deliberately NOT included

- No dictionary database, lesson text, test questions, audio, artwork, animation, font or icon taken from any commercial dictionary application.
- No human pronunciation recordings: audio coverage and per-file licensing on the public archives could not be verified file by file, so pronunciation is produced by the device speech synthesiser and is labelled as synthetic in the app.
- No general character-component (IDS) decomposition data: the upstream file for it is LGPL, and that model was not accepted for this project. The gap is stated in the app instead of being filled with an unlicensed copy.
- No server-side translation, speech scoring, forum, chat or account system: this build is a static web application.
